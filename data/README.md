# Data

`geo_all_channels.csv` is copied unchanged from the Google Meridian repository,
`meridian/data/simulated_data/csv/geo_all_channels.csv` (https://github.com/google/meridian, Apache License 2.0).
It is the simulated geo-level dataset used in Meridian's Getting Started notebook: 40 geos x 156 weeks
(2021-01-25 to 2024-01-15), 5 paid channels with impressions and spend, 1 organic channel, a promotion flag,
2 controls, conversions and revenue per conversion.

The data are **simulated**. They are not any real advertiser's data, and no result here describes a real
company's marketing performance.
